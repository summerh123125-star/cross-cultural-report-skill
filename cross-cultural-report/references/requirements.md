# Requirements Reference (22 CFR §62.10 + GAP)

## Federal regulation (22 CFR §62.10) — cross-cultural activity component

In addition to §62.10, sponsors must require each exchange teacher to complete, within the United States and during each academic year of participation, at least one activity from EACH category:

1. An activity for the teacher's classroom, larger host school or school district, or the community, giving an overview of the history, traditions, heritage, culture, economy, educational system and/or other attributes of the home country. Teachers placed at international schools must also conduct at least one activity per year outside the host school in nearby schools/communities.
2. An activity involving U.S. student dialogue with schools or students in another country (preferably the home country) through virtual exchange or other means, supplementing the in-person exchange.

Annual report must include: teacher full name and sponsor name, and for each activity:
- date(s)
- location
- audience and participants
- general overview including topic
- estimated impact

## GAP program specifics

- Sponsor name: Global Ambassador Programs (GAP).
- Reports required: 2 per year (one Domestic/Local + one Virtual). 6 total for extension years 4 and 5.
- Submission: Google Form in the Teacher Portal; ensure correct form.
- Upload ONE PDF containing the lesson plan/detailed description, evidence and pictures with descriptions.
- Delete the "suggestions for various levels" reference section before submitting.
- Deadline: December 1 (final). Submit early to allow corrections.

## Cross-Cultural Report cover sheet fields (Form 1)

Teacher | Location | Sponsor (Global Ambassador Programs) | Grade | Subject | Activity Date (Month/Day/Year) | Topic | Lesson | Type of Exchange (Domestic/Local Exchange or Virtual Exchange) | Target Audience

## Lesson Plan fields (Form 2)

- Lesson Focus/Goals
- Materials Needed
- Learning Objectives
- Structure/Activities: Preparation / Action / Reflection
- Assessment ("How was this impactful?")

## Domestic report fields (Form 3)

Header: Teacher Name | Sponsor | Type/Name of Activity | Type of Exchange Activity | Date of Activity | Location
Then:
- Include pictures (optional) — with descriptions
- 1. General overview of this cross-cultural activity, including topic and resources used
- 2. Estimated impact of this activity (on the audience and on you as a J-1 teacher)
- 3. Lesson plan to share for the Teacher Resource library: Learning Objectives; Introduction (X minutes); Explicit Instruction / teacher modeling (X minutes); Assessment (X minutes)
- 4. Any other thoughts, suggestions, or feedback on cross-cultural activities

## Virtual report fields (Form 4)

Header: Teacher Name | Sponsor | Type/Name of Activity | Type of Exchange Activity (Virtual) | Partner School (name + country) | Date of Activity | Location (US site + partner site)
Then:
- Include pictures (optional) — with descriptions
- 1. General overview (topic, resources, how the two-way dialogue was structured)
- 2. Estimated impact (on students in both countries and on the teacher)
- 3. Lesson plan for the Teacher Resource library: Session 1 / Session 2 / Session 3 ... each with Learning Objective, Resources, and Activity
- 4. Any other thoughts, suggestions, or feedback
